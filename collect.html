Sign Data Collection

Record short video sequences of each sign to train HEAR's on-device sign classifier. Each recording captures both hands, frame by frame, so the model can learn the motion of a sign — not just a single hand pose. Nothing here is uploaded — sequences are stored in this browser's local storage.

    Manage dataset

    Export your recordings to back them up or share with a collaborator, or import a dataset someone else recorded.

    Importing merges these sequences into your existing dataset — it does not replace or delete anything you already recorded.

    Shared model contribution

    HEAR doesn't have a live server yet, so “shared model” today means: sequences marked as shared can be exported as a separate file, handed to a maintainer, checked over, and merged into a community dataset that gets redistributed for everyone to import. Nothing is uploaded automatically.

      Model accuracy

      Estimated with leave-one-out cross-validation over your recorded sequences: each sequence is compared frame-by-frame against every other sequence, so this reflects real performance on your data — not a placeholder number.

      Unseen-participant accuracy

      This holds out one collector's sequences at a time, trains only on everyone else's, and tests against the held-out person. It answers a different question than the number above: not "does the model remember this data" but "does it generalize to someone it has never seen sign before." Needs at least two collectors' worth of sequences.
