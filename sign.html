Sign to Text

Communicate through signs, words, or both. This experimental tool tracks both hands and recognizes a limited set of signs — including their motion, not just a still hand shape — using your camera.

Prototype notice: This tool recognizes a limited vocabulary of 15 signs, trained on video sequences recorded in your browser. It should not be used as a substitute for professional interpretation, and accuracy depends entirely on the training data you or others provide.
